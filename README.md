# 📊 Mini Marketing Project – Consumer Perception of Himalaya Wellness Products

**Duration:** 14 – 24 September 2025  
**Total Respondents:** 25  
**Method:** Online Google Forms Survey  
**Brand Analyzed:** Himalaya Wellness Products  
**Created by:** Hemavathi V.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

This project analyzes **consumer perception, satisfaction, and usage patterns** of Himalaya Wellness Products, a leading Indian herbal personal care brand.  
The study is based on a quantitative survey conducted among 25 respondents aged 19–37.

The project includes market segmentation, targeting, brand perception analysis, reasons for switching, and user feedback.

---

## 🎯 Objectives

- To understand consumer attitudes toward Himalaya products  
- To evaluate satisfaction levels among current and past users  
- To identify factors influencing product choice and switching behavior  
- To analyze brand positioning and competitive environment

---

## 🧪 Research Methodology

- **Data Source:** Primary data through Google Forms  
- **Sampling Method:** Convenience sampling  
- **Target Group:** Urban & semi-urban young adults  
- **Total Responses:** 25  
- **Tools:** Google Forms, Google Sheets

---

## 🧩 Market Segmentation

| Segmentation Type | Details |
|-------------------|---------|
| **Demographic** | Age 19–37; 60% Female, 40% Male |
| **Behavioral** | 18 past users, 8 current users, 1 non-user |
| **Psychographic** | Preference for herbal & budget-friendly products |
| **Geographic** | Urban / semi-urban |

---

## 🎯 Targeting Strategy

Himalaya adopts a **differentiated targeting strategy**, focusing on:

- Herbal & health-conscious consumers  
- Skincare & wellness categories  
- Adults, parents, and families  
- Both genders (female-majority audience)

---

## 🏁 Key Findings

### ⭐ Brand Perception
- **Positive:** 20  
- **Neutral:** 4  
- **Negative:** 1  

Consumers appreciate herbal ingredients, trust, and affordability.

---

### ⭐ Customer Satisfaction (24 users)
- **Excellent / Very Good:** 14 (58%)  
- **Average:** 6  
- **Neutral:** 3  
- **Poor:** 1  

---

### ⭐ Most Used Himalaya Products
- Neem Face Wash – 14  
- Hair Oil/Shampoo – 8  
- Toothpaste – 5  
- Baby Care – 2  
- Wellness products – 1  

---

### ⭐ Why Users Choose Himalaya
- Herbal/Natural – 11  
- Brand Trust – 11  
- Affordable – 8  
- Recommendations – 6  

---

### ⭐ Why Past Users Switched
- No visible results – 5  
- Wanted to try new brands – 3  
- Found better alternatives – 2  
- Skin issues – 1  
- Price concerns – 1  
- Social media influence – 2  

---

## 📝 Feedback from Users
- Improve effectiveness (especially shampoos)  
- More fragrance options  
- Better availability  
- Maintain herbal purity

## 📘 Conclusion

Himalaya enjoys strong brand trust and positive perception, driven by its herbal positioning.  
However, the brand faces challenges related to product effectiveness and competition from newer herbal brands.  
Despite some switching behavior, many users still recommend Himalaya, showing that **brand loyalty remains strong**.
